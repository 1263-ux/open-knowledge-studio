## What this batch proves

- Five longer tasks left concrete file, report, decision, receipt, reply, or commit evidence;
  all five ended with a usable result and `context_reask_count=0`.
- Claude interactive is now independently proven and must not be conflated with the
  earlier Claude headless partial result. The native runner/package-root fix is sufficient
  for this tested path.
- Session A/B handoff is workable as a durable Thread plus explicit receipt and reply, but
  host quota, prompt execution, and accidental operator actions can still derail a run.
- Git-backed coordination works as a validation-only local mirror with explicit commit
  boundaries. It does not justify adding a transport daemon or wake mechanism.

## Decision

P3.2 acceptance is met. Keep the Mail protocol frozen. The next step is to accumulate
real failure events and measure whether the zero-context-reask signal holds; do not add
fields, lifecycle states, wake, daemon, WebSocket, Redis, MQ, or cross-machine
productization before the failure threshold and a new Owner decision.
